    The system we live in

    A thin disc of leftover starlight

    Eight planets, more than two hundred moons, a belt of rubble between Mars and Jupiter, ice beyond Neptune. Light needs four hours to reach Neptune and 8 minutes 20 seconds to reach you. The heliopause, where the solar wind gives up, sits near 120 AU. Voyager 1 is already past it.

    Habitable

    Worlds we could try to live on

    None of these is Earth. A few are close in size and sit in the zone where liquid water can exist. Mars is the only one a ship can reach in a human lifetime. The rest are light-years out.

    Sol · rocky

    Mars

    From Earth54.6–401 million km
    Light3 to 22 min
    Size0.53 × Earth
    Gravity0.38 g

    Day is 24h 37m. Ice at the poles. Thin CO2 air, no magnetic shield. Closest world we can actually land on and build under.

    Alpha Centauri · HZ

    Proxima b

    From Earth4.24 ly · 40.1 trillion km
    Light4.24 years
    Size~1.17 × Earth mass
    Orbit11.2 days

    Nearest exoplanet in a habitable zone. Likely tidally locked. The red dwarf flares. Still the shortest jump off our Sun.

    TRAPPIST-1 · HZ

    TRAPPIST-1e

    From Earth40.5 ly · 383 trillion km
    Light40.5 years
    Size0.91 × Earth
    Orbit6.1 days

    Earth-sized, rocky, in the liquid-water belt of a cool dwarf. Six other worlds in the same system. Density looks like rock, not gas.

    Kepler-186 · HZ

    Kepler-186f

    From Earth579 ly · 5.48 quadrillion km
    Light579 years
    Size1.17 × Earth
    Orbit130 days

    First Earth-size planet found in a habitable zone. Red-dwarf host. Could hold surface water if it kept an atmosphere.

    TOI-700 · HZ

    TOI-700 d

    From Earth101 ly · 956 trillion km
    Light101 years
    Size1.19 × Earth
    Orbit37.4 days

    TESS find, later confirmed. Receives about 86% of the starlight Earth gets. Quiet M dwarf, so an atmosphere might survive.

    From Earth1,206 ly · 11.4 quadrillion km

    One of the highest Earth-similarity scores in the catalog. K-star light, cooler than the Sun. Super-Earth, still in the water belt.

    Local group

    Galaxies around the Sun

    00

    Milky Way

    Barred spiral. We sit in the Orion arm, 26,000 light-years from Sagittarius A*, a black hole of 4.3 million Suns. One galactic year is 230 million Earth years. About 100–400 billion stars. 100,000 light-years across.

    01

    Andromeda

    M31. 2.47 million light-years. Closes at 110 km/s. In 4.5 billion years the two discs merge. Larger than us. A trillion stars. Already spans three degrees of sky — wider than six full moons.

    02

    Triangulum

    M33. The third spiral of the Local Group, 2.73 million light-years out. 40 billion stars. A satellite of Andromeda, and likely coming with her.
